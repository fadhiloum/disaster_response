Disaster Response Platform

A coordination workspace for emergency teams to manage programs, field needs, resources, partner activity, maps, budgets, and situation reports from one operational picture.

Problem

Disaster response teams often work across scattered spreadsheets, chat threads, map screenshots, and document drafts. That slows down decisions, hides resource gaps, and makes it hard to keep situation reports current while conditions are changing.

Solution

The app centralizes response programs, needs, tasks, resources, teams, partner 3W activity, map layers, budget controls, and SitRep drafting behind role-aware workflows and shared API/data layers.

One Result

Coordinators can generate, review, save, audit, and export SitReps from live program data instead of rebuilding reports manually.

Operational, donor, and executive SitRep exports are now available as text or PDF.
